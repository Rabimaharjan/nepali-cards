NEPALI CARDS WEBSITE - SETUP

1. Name your 20 recordings n01 ... n20 (keep the ending: .m4a, .mp3 or .wav) and put them in the "audio" folder.
   Match the number on each card:
   n01 Namaste | n02 How are you? | n03 I am fine | n04 Mum | n05 Dad
   n06 Grandmother | n07 Grandfather | n08 Older brother | n09 Older sister | n10 I want water
   n11 I am hungry | n12 Let's eat | n13 I am sleepy | n14 Thank you | n15 Sorry / Excuse me
   n16 Please | n17 Yes / No | n18 Good night | n19 See you again | n20 I love you (to elders)

2. Upload index.html and the audio folder to a new PUBLIC GitHub repository, then turn on GitHub Pages
   (Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > / (root)).

3. Your site will be at https://USERNAME.github.io/REPO-NAME/
   Each card has its own link: https://USERNAME.github.io/REPO-NAME/?c=n01  (n01 to n20)
   Those links are what the QR codes on the printed cards will open.
